Google Calendar & Tasks — Setup Guide

One-time setup (~5 min). Enables Google Login and real-time Calendar & Tasks sync.

Step-by-step

    Authorized Redirect URI

    Google Cloud Console strictly matches this URL character-for-character:

    ⚠️ Seeing "Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch"?

    In Google Cloud Console → Credentials, click your OAuth 2.0 Client ID, scroll down to Authorized redirect URIs, click + ADD URI, and add:

    http://localhost:3000/api/google/oauth/callback http://127.0.0.1:3000/api/google/oauth/callback http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/google/oauth/callback

    After connecting

    • Log in with Google with one click
    • Calendar events and tasks sync automatically
    • Reminders fire 10 min, 5 min before, and at event time with custom sounds
    • Snooze from the desktop/in-app alert popup (5 / 10 / 30 / 60 min)
    • Silence any event from the Google tab without affecting others
